    Frequently asked questions

    Is CoverKit really free?

    Yes — free forever, open source, no sign-up and no watermark on your PDF. If it saved your morning, a star on GitHub helps other NSUers find it.

    How do I find my course?

    Start typing in Course name — a few letters of the name (try “linear”) or the code itself (MAT125). Pick your course from the list and the code fills in with it. Prefer codes? Type the code under Course code and the name appears by itself. If your course is not listed yet, just type it in: CoverKit remembers it in your browser for next time, and a note through Feedback gets it added for everyone.

    What can I make besides assignment covers?

    Lab reports, project reports, term papers and plain reports — pick the type under Category & Template and add its number (“Lab Report 3”) beside it. Then choose from six cover styles: Classic, Minimal, Formal, Academic, Frame or Arch.

    Can I change the colors or the logo?

    Yes. Under Category & Template, pick one of five colors (black & white, NSU blue, forest, maroon or teal) and it recolors whichever template you chose. The Logo menu switches the NSU seal between full color and black & white — handy if you plan to print in grayscale.

    Can I make one cover for my whole group?

    Yes. Under Submitted by, tap + Add group member for up to five people. Names, student IDs and serials print in the same order you type them.

    Can I print a blank cover and fill it in by hand?

    Of course. Leave the form empty and press Download PDF — you get a clean cover with the labels printed and room to write beside them.

    Do I need an account? Where do my details go?

    No account, ever. What you type stays in this browser, on this device, so the form is ready on your next visit — your name, ID and course details are never sent to anyone. Reset clears it, and if you cleared by mistake, Undo brings it back.

    The only thing the site counts is that a cover was downloaded and which design was used, so I can tell which designs are worth improving. Nothing about you is attached to that count.

    Does it work on my phone, or offline?

    Both. CoverKit is built for phones as much as laptops, and after your first visit it works offline — add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app.

    PDF download or print — which should I use?

    Download PDF gives you a file to submit or print anywhere. Already at a printer? Press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on a Mac) and only the cover itself prints, on A4.
